Privacy notice

Last updated November 2026

Who we are

Tech Atelier builds AI agents for UK businesses. For anything about your data, email hello@tech-atelier.co.uk.

What we collect

When you use the contact form or the free check on our website, we collect what you choose to give us:

  • your name and email address
  • your business name, if you add it
  • your message and your answers to the free check

If you message us on WhatsApp or email us directly, we receive whatever you send. We don't use cookies, analytics or tracking on this site.

Why we use it

Only to reply to you and talk about the work you've asked about. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: you've contacted us and expect a reply. We won't add you to a mailing list or pass your details to anyone for marketing.

Where it's kept

Form submissions are delivered to our business email by Resend, and our email is hosted by Namecheap Private Email. The website is hosted by Vercel. All three act as our processors.

How long we keep it

If you don't become a client, we delete your enquiry within 24 months of our last contact. If you do, we keep what we need for the length of our contract and as the law requires afterwards.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us and we'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
